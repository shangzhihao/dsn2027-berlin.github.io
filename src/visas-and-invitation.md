## Visa Information

Visa requirements depend on your nationality, the purpose of your visit, and
the length of your stay. The German Federal Foreign Office provides the
official resources for determining which requirements apply to you:

- <a href="https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/en/visa-service/visa-navigator-2489244" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">German Federal Foreign Office Visa Navigator</a>
- <a href="https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/en/visa-service/231148-231148" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Overview of visa requirements and exemptions</a>
- <a href="https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/en/about-us/auslandsvertretungen/deutsche-auslandsvertretungen" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Find a German mission abroad</a>

Please allow sufficient time for appointments and processing. Visa decisions
are made solely by the responsible German authorities; conference registration
or an invitation letter does not guarantee that a visa will be issued.

## Invitation Letters

DSN 2027 plans to provide invitation letters for eligible conference
participants who need supporting documentation for a visa application. An
invitation letter confirms participation in the conference but does not provide
financial support or replace the visa application process.

Applicants should expect to provide information such as:

- full name exactly as shown in the passport
- passport number and country of issue
- postal address and institutional affiliation
- role at DSN 2027, including accepted paper or presentation details when
  applicable
- conference registration details
