The Rising Star in Dependability Award is presented annually at DSN to a junior
researcher from academia or industry who demonstrates outstanding potential for
creative ideas and innovative research. It is jointly sponsored by the IEEE
Technical Community on Dependable Computing and Fault Tolerance and IFIP
Working Group 10.4 on Dependable Computing and Fault Tolerance.

## Eligibility and Nominations

Under the current award rules, candidates must have graduated no more than ten
years before the nomination deadline, with career disruptions or delays taken
into consideration by the selection committee. Self-nominations are not
accepted.

The permanent award page provides the complete nomination package, eligibility
rules, and selection criteria.
