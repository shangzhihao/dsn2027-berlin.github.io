## About the Program

The mentoring program is intended to make DSN more welcoming and useful for
students, early-career researchers, and participants attending the conference
for the first time. Mentees are matched with experienced members of the DSN
community who can share advice, answer questions, and help introduce them to
other participants.

Mentors and mentees are encouraged to meet during the conference. They may also
choose to connect online before or after DSN 2027.

## Who Can Participate?

- **Mentees:** students, early-career researchers, and first-time DSN attendees
- **Mentors:** experienced researchers and practitioners willing to share their
  perspective and help participants engage with the community

Participation is voluntary. The matching process will consider the interests
and preferences submitted by prospective mentors and mentees.
