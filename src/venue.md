## Tagungswerk

**Lindenstraße 85**<br>
**10969 Berlin, Germany**

<a href="https://besondere-orte.com/en/venues/tagungswerk" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Visit the official Tagungswerk website</a>

Tagungswerk is located in central Berlin between the Jewish Museum Berlin and
Checkpoint Charlie.

## Getting There

- **Underground:** U6 to Kochstraße/Checkpoint Charlie, followed by approximately seven minutes on foot
- **S-Bahn:** S1, S2, S25, or S26 to Anhalter Bahnhof, followed by approximately fifteen minutes on foot
- **Bus:** 248 to Jüdisches Museum or M29 to Lindenstraße/Oranienstraße
