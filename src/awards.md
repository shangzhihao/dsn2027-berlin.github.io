## DSN 2027 Awards

DSN presents conference paper and artifact awards and hosts several awards that
recognize broader contributions to the dependability community.

### Best Paper Awards

DSN 2027 will give three Best Paper Awards. One of the three papers will also
receive the Distinguished Best Paper Award based on the quality of its oral
presentation. The candidates are selected from accepted papers through the
process described in the [Call for Contributions](/call-for-contributions/#awards).

### Community Awards

- [William C. Carter Ph.D. Dissertation Award](/awards/carter-award/) — recognizes a significant contribution made through Ph.D. dissertation research
- [Rising Star in Dependability Award](/awards/rising-star-award/) — recognizes a junior researcher with outstanding potential for creative and innovative work
- [Test-of-Time Award](/awards/test-of-time-award/) — recognizes DSN papers with lasting impact
- [Distinguished Artifact Award](/awards/distinguished-artifact-award/) — recognizes an outstanding artifact associated with an accepted DSN paper
- [Jean-Claude Laprie Award](/awards/jean-claude-laprie-award/) — recognizes influential work that has shaped the dependability field
