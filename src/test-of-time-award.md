The DSN Test-of-Time Award recognizes papers published at DSN ten years earlier
that have made a lasting contribution. The award normally recognizes two papers;
the permanent award rules describe the exceptional circumstances in which the
eligible publication window or number of awards may differ.

## Nominations and Selection

Nominations may be made by anyone familiar with the paper. The award committee
assesses impact through holistic peer review, supported by evidence appropriate
to the work's academic, educational, technical, or industrial context.
