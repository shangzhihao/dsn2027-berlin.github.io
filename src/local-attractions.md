## Plan Your Visit

Opening hours, admission requirements, and temporary closures may change.
Please check each attraction’s official website before visiting. For route
planning and current public transport information, use the
<a href="https://www.bvg.de/en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">BVG website</a>.
