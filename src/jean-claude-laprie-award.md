The Jean-Claude Laprie Award in Dependable Computing recognizes outstanding
papers that have had a significant impact on the theory or practice of
dependability. Eligible work must have been published at least ten years before
the award year.

The award may recognize technical or scientific impact, industrial or
commercial impact, or contribution to the dependable-computing community.
