DSN encourages authors of accepted papers to make code and datasets publicly
available to support reproducibility, replicability, and future research.

Artifacts associated with accepted papers may be submitted to the DSN 2027
artifact evaluation track. They are evaluated by a dedicated Artifact
Evaluation Committee through a single-blind review process. All submitted
artifacts compete for the Distinguished Artifact Award, which is decided by
that committee.

See the [Call for Contributions](/call-for-contributions/#artifact-evaluation)
for the current overview of the artifact evaluation process.

  <strong>Further details:</strong> Submission instructions, dates, and the
  award recipient will be published when available.
